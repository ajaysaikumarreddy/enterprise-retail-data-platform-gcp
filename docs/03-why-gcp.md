# Why Google Cloud Platform?

## Business Requirement

C&S Grocers requires a scalable analytics platform capable of processing data from multiple operational systems.

## Why GCP?

- Managed cloud services
- Excellent integration between GCS, BigQuery, Dataproc, and Composer
- Highly scalable architecture
- Strong analytics ecosystem
- Serverless data warehouse (BigQuery)
- Cost-effective storage with Google Cloud Storage
- Easy integration with Power BI
- Supports future AI and Machine Learning initiatives

## Why not directly use MySQL?

MySQL is designed for transactional processing (OLTP). Running analytical queries directly on operational databases impacts business transactions and application performance.

## Why BigQuery?

BigQuery is an analytical data warehouse optimized for large-scale reporting, aggregations, and business intelligence. It enables centralized, standardized, and governed reporting across departments.
