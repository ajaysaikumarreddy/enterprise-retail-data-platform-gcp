# Interview Notes - Day 1

## Question

Why do companies build Data Platforms?

### Answer

To centralize data from multiple systems, improve data quality, create a single source of truth, enable analytics, automate reporting, and support business decision making.

---

## Question

Why can't companies use Excel?

### Answer

Excel cannot efficiently handle enterprise-scale data, concurrent users, automated pipelines, governance, historical tracking, or real-time analytics.

---

## Question

Why is a Single Source of Truth important?

### Answer

A single source of truth ensures that all departments use the same validated data, reducing inconsistencies and improving confidence in business reports.
