# Business Problem

## Company

C&S Grocers is a wholesale grocery distributor that manages products, inventory, suppliers, customers, and retail orders.

## Existing Challenges

- Multiple disconnected source systems
- Duplicate customer and product records
- Inconsistent reporting across departments
- Manual Excel reporting
- Delayed decision making
- Lack of centralized data platform
- Poor data quality
- No historical tracking
- Difficult troubleshooting

## Business Goals

- Build a centralized enterprise data platform
- Improve data quality
- Create a single source of truth
- Automate reporting
- Enable near real-time analytics
- Reduce manual effort
- Improve operational efficiency

## Expected Benefits

- Faster reporting
- Improved business decisions
- Better customer insights
- Inventory optimization
- Supplier performance tracking
- Enterprise data governance
